REAL HISTORY IN THIS VIDEO
• The Last Glacial Maximum was roughly 26,000-19,000 years ago
• Hypothermia can become life-threatening within hours in cold, wet exposure
• Upper Palaeolithic people lived in small hunter-gatherer bands hunting large game
• Ice Age diets in cold regions relied heavily on animal foods, supplemented by plants and fish
• Mammoth-bone dwellings are known from sites such as Mezhyrich, Ukraine
• Eyed bone needles appear in the Upper Palaeolithic
• Ice Age Europe had cave lions, wolves and cave bears
• The Great Pyramid of Giza was built c. 2560 BC (reign of Khufu)
• Archaeology at Giza indicates the pyramid builders were organized, paid and fed laborers, not slaves
• The workers' town at Giza had bakeries and breweries; rations included bread and beer
• Skeletons of Giza workers show healed fractures, indicating medical care
• Work-gang names such as 'Friends of Khufu' appear in graffiti at Giza

Stan's story is a dramatization; the historical facts are real, simplified for the video, and sourced below. Numbers are estimates — historians argue about the details, and that's part of the fun.
